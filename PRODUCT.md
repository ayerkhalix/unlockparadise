# Unlock Paradise

Unlock Paradise helps travelers discover Places in Palawan, save a Bucketlist, record visits and arrange day-by-day itineraries. Existing maps, destination photography and the original brand are the visual foundation.

The product serves English-speaking travelers in the Philippine market. Currency is PHP; date display uses English Philippine conventions and Asia/Manila when time zones matter. Explorer and Explorer+ access, staff roles and MFA are enforced by the existing Next.js/Supabase server and database policies.

The merchandise collection uses the project’s actual products, variant information, photographs and prices. A browser-local shopping cart holds selections. Online ordering and payment collection remain closed. Bag additions and browsing activity are not sales.

Admin Studio is an operating workspace for existing Places, merchandise, orders, measured analytics, members, moderation and settings. Its priority is clear actions and reliable editing. The authorized Phase 3.1 refinement adds the existing official logo, navy-default dark and persistent warm light themes, and simpler summaries; it does not change permissions or financial workflows.

Design authority: the user’s supplied Phase 3.1 brief, existing public brand assets and DESIGN.md. Runtime CSS owns the accepted tokens. Preserve factual content and legal copy; do not invent offers, reviews, metrics, travel facts or stock availability.

Phase 3.1 success means readable shared card spacing, a product-led editorial storefront, actual measured plane/product travel with accessible fallbacks, and coherent administration at phone through large desktop widths. Saves must confirm persistence before success; variants, quantities and owner/version isolation remain authoritative.

Work is local against pinned synthetic staging test identities. No production deployment, live WordPress writes, schema migration, bulk media upload or checkout activation is part of this phase. Browser audits cover shared templates and representative records, not every destination or every assistive technology.
