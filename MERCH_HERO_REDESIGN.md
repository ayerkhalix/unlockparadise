# Merchandise hero

Two internal browser prototypes were captured at 1440 and 390px in `artifacts/phase3-1/hero-directions/` before selecting a final layout. These are development evidence, not alternative shipped components.

| Direction | Composition | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — selected | Copy beside a dominant product photograph; stacked on phones | Full folded tee and print remain recognizable; CTA and image share the first desktop screen |
| B | Full-width lookbook image below a wide title/action row | Strong image scale but the shallow desktop crop cuts garment context and pushes browsing further down |

The selected hero uses the first actual published catalog product’s name, color and display image. Existing tee photography supplies the initial composition. “Keep Palawan close.” accompanies one collection CTA and one short descriptive sentence. No prices, offers, testimonials or origin/manufacturing claims were invented.

The mountain banner, repeated uppercase marketing labels, overlay gradient and decorative rounded hero frame were removed. Outfit headings and Open Sans body/controls remain consistent with the travel site. Product browsing, search/categories, related products and ordering safeguards remain intact. Catalog cards and product details share the same validated Add to bag form behavior and flight adapter.

No new raster assets were generated or edited. Existing official logo and product photographs are reused in place; their provenance is the project’s supplied original/merch asset library.
